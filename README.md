# Walmart Sales Forecasting and Inventory Management

Group BI project, Humber Polytechnic (BIA 5401).

## The question

Retail inventory goes wrong in two directions. Too much stock ties up cash, too little loses the sale. Can historical sales tell us what to stock and when?

## Approach

1. Explored historical Walmart sales, checking for seasonality and trend
2. Tested forecasting approaches against a holdout period rather than fitting on everything
3. Compared accuracy across methods
4. Translated the chosen forecast into inventory recommendations

## Tools

Python, pandas, statsmodels, PowerPoint
